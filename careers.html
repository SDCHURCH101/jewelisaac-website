Why Jewel Isaac

Real work. Real craft.
Real careers.

A lot of contractors hand the work to someone else. We don't. Because we self-perform the critical path, our crews build the kind of hands-on experience that turns laborers into foremen and foremen into superintendents. Add an owner with two decades in the trade, deep Interior roots, and our Minto Global North partnership, and you get steady, meaningful work close to home.

What we offer

More than a paycheck.

We invest in the people who do the work, on site and in the office.

Competitive Pay

Wages that reflect your skill, certifications, and the value you bring to the job, reviewed as you grow.

Health & Medical

Medical coverage options to keep you and your family taken care of, on and off the clock.

Retirement Savings

Build your own future, not just ours, with retirement savings that travel with you.

Paid Time Off

PTO and paid holidays so the time you spend off the job is yours to keep.

Training & Advancement

On-the-job training, certifications, and a clear path from apprentice to foreman to superintendent.

Per Diem & Travel

Per diem, lodging, and travel covered on remote, Slope, and off-the-road-system assignments.

Year-Round Work

A steady pipeline of Interior and statewide projects that keeps crews working through the winter.

Tools & Gear

Company tools, equipment, and the PPE to do the job right and do it safely.

Zero-Harm Culture

A genuine safety-first jobsite where stopping unsafe work is expected, not punished.
