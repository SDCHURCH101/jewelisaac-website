What we bring to your team

Built to make your bid stronger.

Whether you need a subcontractor, a self-perform teaming partner, or an 8(a) sole-source path, Jewel Isaac brings the crews, the credentials, and the Alaska logistics that move a job from award to closeout.

Local self-perform crews

About 25 Alaska-based staff who self-perform carpentry, framing, drywall and finishes, roofing, siding, and casework. We hold the critical path in-house instead of stacking subs, which protects your schedule and your margin.

Remote & Arctic logistics

We plan and execute around barge windows, ice roads, and air freight, and we mobilize crews and materials to the Interior, the North Slope, and off-road village sites where most contractors will not go.

SAM & federal registration

SAM.gov registered and ready to be added to your team: UEI ULLZLBKHGCB8, CAGE 7PYR4, Alaska General Contractor License #115766, and primary NAICS 236220. We know the documentation discipline agencies expect.

Minto Global North 8(a) teaming

We team with Minto Global North, an SBA-approved 8(a) Alaska Native Corporation. That opens a sole-source, direct-award path for federal agencies with no competitive cap and an award that cannot be protested.

Safety documentation

An OSHA-aligned program with daily job-hazard analysis, toolbox training, stop-work authority for every worker, and incident review on every event. Safety records and program documents are available on request for prequalification.

Fast, responsive estimating

Send the scope, site, and schedule and our estimating team responds promptly with budgets, scopes, and teaming letters, so you can hit your proposal deadline with a partner already locked in.
