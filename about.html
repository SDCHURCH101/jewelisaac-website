Who we are

Our story

Built here. Built to stay.

Jewel Isaac, LLC is a Fairbanks-headquartered general contractor with an Anchorage office and crews working statewide across Alaska.

We started where the work is hardest: the Interior and the remote sites that other contractors fly over. Founded in 2016 and led by an owner who has built in Alaska since 2000, we've grown into a multi-market construction company self-performing commercial, government, industrial, mining, and oil & gas projects, without losing the thing that got us here.

That thing is relationships. Owners come back to us because we're straight about scope and schedule, because our crews take pride in the work, and because we stand behind it after the punch list is signed.

Our roots in Interior Alaska, including our Minto Global North partnership, give us a genuinely local advantage on the projects that matter most to the communities we serve.

What we stand for

Four things we don't compromise.

Relationships first

Repeat owners and references are our best marketing. We earn the next job by how we run this one.

Self-perform control

Owning the critical path with our own crews means fewer handoffs, tighter schedules, and accountability that doesn't get subcontracted away.

Zero-harm safety

Everyone goes home. Our zero-harm culture reflects disciplined planning, trained crews, and field leadership that stops work before conditions turn.

Local roots

Alaska-based mobilization, Interior knowledge, and our Minto Global North partnership keep costs down and outcomes in the community.

Minto Global North
Partnership

Rooted in Interior Alaska.

Through our Minto Global North partnership, Jewel Isaac brings authentic local presence and an Alaska-Native connection to the projects we pursue, an advantage for owners and agencies who value working with teams that are part of the communities they build in.

It shapes how we hire, how we mobilize, and how we partner on federal and resource-sector work. If your project has local-presence or teaming requirements, let's talk about how we fit.
