How we deliver

Built your way.

We work in whatever delivery method fits your project and procurement, from a single design-build contract to competitive hard bids and federal task orders.

01

Design-Build

One contract for design and construction, with a single point of accountability and a faster path from concept to occupancy.

02

CM at Risk

We join early as construction manager for budgeting and constructability, then deliver as builder under a guaranteed maximum price.

03

Hard-Bid

Competitive lump-sum bids on complete drawings, the traditional design-bid-build path for public and private work.

04

IDIQ / Task Order / JOC

Indefinite-delivery contracts and job orders for recurring facility renovations and repairs, mobilized fast with minimal mission impact.

05

Negotiated / T&M

Negotiated or time-and-materials work for fast-track, emergency, and evolving scopes where speed and flexibility matter most.

Preconstruction

De-risked before the first shovel.

On negotiated and design-build work we bring preconstruction to the table early: budgeting and estimating, constructability review, scheduling and phasing, value engineering, and subcontractor and supplier buyout, so surprises are designed out before the field work starts.
